The interactive prompt currently lives only on the homepage (`src/content/docs/index.mdx`). Acceptance: ↑/↓ recall prior commands; Tab completes `in`→`install` and `cd h`→`cd hop`; ambiguous Tab lists candidates; Ctrl-L clears; Ctrl-C cancels the line.
